- **Verified:** the relevant text was read in the source cited.
- **Partly verified:** only part of the source was read; the unread part is identified.
- **Secondary:** commentary used for context, not as authority for what a law or licence says.
- **Heading only:** the provision's existence and title were confirmed, but not its text.
