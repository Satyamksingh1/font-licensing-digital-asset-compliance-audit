## Full version (for a CV project section)

**Font Licensing & Digital Asset Compliance Audit: A Simulated Legal Review** | Independent research project | October 2026
GitHub: `font-licensing-digital-asset-compliance-audit`

- Designed a fictional case study (an Indian apparel brand with no licence records) and analysed font use across packaging, web, app, digital marketing, agency, cloud and internal channels.
- Reviewed Monotype's published License and Service Terms (Version 2.0) and a legacy Monotype EULA as reference frameworks, mapping desktop, server, web, application and digital marketing grants to each channel with pinpoint citations.
- Analysed the Copyright Act, 1957 on computer programmes, licences and infringement (ss. 2(ffc), 14(b), 30A, 51(a)(i), 52(1)(aa)), identifying open questions on protection of font files and typefaces in India.
- Produced a prioritised findings register, evidence register, use-case matrix, licence verification checklist and phased remediation plan.

*All facts are fictional. No client engagement took place and the project has no affiliation with Monotype or any font company.*
